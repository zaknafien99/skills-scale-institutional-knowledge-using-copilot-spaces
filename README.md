📝 Process Summary
OctoAcme follows a clear, actionable project management lifecycle:

Prioritize clarity in project scope and requirements
Plan collaboratively with clear milestones and risk anticipation
Regular check-ins during execution to track progress
Ongoing risk and communication management
Thorough review before release and opportunities for continuous improvement after delivery
Clearly defined roles and responsibilities for all participants
